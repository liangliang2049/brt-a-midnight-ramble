## v134 — Journey fixes & travel albums / 旅程修复与风景相册

2026-11-06

- Prevents a progression softlock caused by driving past the fox and Shuangshi Night School without stopping. Compatible affected saves return to the unfinished school visit while keeping collected items and photos.
- Keeps the gift reveal at its intended point in the story; forgotten-gift reminders appear only after it is offered.
- Separates landscape snapshots from group photos and mementos. Press **P** to save a landscape shot, then **T → 旅行风景相册** to view or export the original PNG. Keeps the existing photo-printing story sequence.
- Restores the Bobo souvenir in the school replay and repairs missing souvenirs only where a save confirms prior collection.

- 修复不停经过小狐狸、跳过双十夜校后可能卡住旅程的问题；受影响的兼容旧存档会接回尚未完成的双十环节，保留已有物品和照片。
- 礼物提示不再提前剧透，只在送礼剧情后提醒领取。
- 后备箱分出旅行风景相册与合影／纪念照。**P 拍风景自动保存，T → 旅行风景相册随时查看或导出原图**；保留原有打印照片与送礼流程。
- 补齐双十重玩入口的波波纪念品；普通存档仅在明确已有领取记录时补回遗漏。

New players: download **BRT_A_Midnight_Ramble_Season_One_Windows_v134.zip**, extract the entire ZIP and run **开始游戏.cmd**. Windows 64-bit; Chinese interface and dialogue; no Godot installation required.

第一次玩：下载完整 v134 ZIP，完整解压后运行 **开始游戏.cmd**。Windows 64 位，中文界面与对话，无需安装 Godot。

The patch requires the complete v133 package and does not include or replace saves.
